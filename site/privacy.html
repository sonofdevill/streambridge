Privacy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

StreamBridge runs on your own computer. It doesn't collect, send or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

What's stored on your computer

Your settings, including any stream keys you enter, are saved only in a file on your computer:

  • Windows: %AppData%\StreamBridge\config.json
  • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/StreamBridge/config.json
  • Linux: ~/.config/StreamBridge/config.json

What the app connects to

  • The platforms you choose (such as Kick, YouTube or Twitch), to send your stream.
  • Public DNS servers (1.1.1.1 and 8.8.8.8), to answer the PS5's other lookups.
  • In Iran mode, your own proxy (for example V2ray) on the same computer.
  • OBS or Streamlabs on the same computer, only when you press an Add button.

The app doesn't check for updates or send crash reports anywhere.

Phone control

Phone control is off until you turn it on. When it's on, it's only reachable on your home network and needs the 6-digit PIN or the QR code.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics or trackers. It only remembers the language you pick, in your own browser. Like any website, the server may keep IP addresses and request times for a short while for security.
