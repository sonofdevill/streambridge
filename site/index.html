One click into OBS or Streamlabs

Press “Add to OBS” and the PS5 appears in your current scene, fitted to the screen and placed under your overlays. Keep your webcam, alerts and chat exactly as they are.

  • Works with OBS 28 and newer, and Streamlabs Desktop
  • Picture guides with arrows for the one-time setup

Check on it from your phone

Scan a QR code and the control panel opens on your phone. See whether you're live, switch Kick or YouTube on and off, all from the couch.

  • Off until you turn it on
  • Protected by a 6-digit PIN, only on your home Wi-Fi

Iran mode, for when Twitch is blocked

The PS5 has to reach Twitch to sign in and start a broadcast, and you can't run a VPN on it. Connect V2ray on your computer and switch on Iran mode. StreamBridge passes only the PS5's Twitch traffic through it, still encrypted.

  • Nothing to install or change on the PS5 besides DNS
  • Finds v2rayN, NekoRay, Hiddify or Clash on its own

Seeing “Windows protected your PC”?

Windows shows this box for new apps it hasn't seen many times yet. It isn't a virus warning. Two clicks and you won't see it again for this file.

Then allow it through the firewall

Windows Firewall asks once whether StreamBridge may use the network. Click Allow, with Private networks ticked. Without this, the PS5 can't reach your computer.

Want to check the file is really ours?

Open PowerShell in your Downloads folder and run Get-FileHash StreamBridge-1.3-Windows-x64.zip. The result should match the SHA-256 shown under the Windows download.

Questions

Do I really not need a capture card?

Really. The PS5 already encodes its broadcast for Twitch. StreamBridge just catches it on your computer instead of letting it go to Twitch, so you get the PS5's own picture without a cable or capture card.

Do I need a Twitch account?

Yes. The PS5 only shows Go Live once a Twitch account is linked (Settings, Users and Accounts, Linked Services). Nothing is sent to Twitch unless you switch on “Also send to my Twitch” in StreamBridge.

How much delay is there?

Under two seconds from the PS5 to your computer. Kick, YouTube or Twitch add their own delay on top, the same as with any stream.

Will it slow down my internet or other devices?

No. StreamBridge only answers the PS5's question about where Twitch's video server is. Every other lookup is passed to normal DNS (1.1.1.1), and only the PS5 uses StreamBridge as its DNS.

Does the computer have to stay on?

Yes, while you stream. When you stop using StreamBridge for good, set the PS5's DNS back to Automatic.

Where is my stream key kept?

Only on your computer, in StreamBridge's settings file. It isn't sent anywhere except to the platform it belongs to.

Does it work in Iran?

Yes. Turn on Iran mode in Settings and keep V2ray connected on the same computer. The PS5 only needs its DNS pointed at your computer, as usual.

What about PS4 or Xbox?

StreamBridge is built and tested for the PS5. Other consoles that stream to Twitch might work too, but we haven't tested them.

Why does Windows warn me about it?

Windows warns about any new app that hasn't been downloaded many times yet. See the two clicks that get past it.
